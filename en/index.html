How we work

  • We start from a concrete case.

    Each product starts with a task of a person or an organization.

  • A person makes the important decisions.

    Hannah asks for permission before acting on the computer. In Complia, an approval requires a person’s signature.

  • We publish code when we can.

    Hannah and her motion model are MIT-licensed.

Kbit Slot · warehouses

k·kbitslot

Product slotting in warehouses.

It calculates where to place each product from the warehouse layout, the orders, the current locations and the shift rules. Three engines propose a layout: a classical one, an exact one and a quantum one. All proposals go through the same validation and become a task plan.

Problem
Quadratic assignment (QAP)
Engines
Classical, exact (MILP) and quantum (QAOA with Qiskit)
Result
Task plan for the shift
